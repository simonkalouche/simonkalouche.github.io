Simon Kalouche

Founder and CEO of Nimble. Nimble builds autonomous industries powered by general superhumanoids. We've developed the world's first general warehouse superhumanoid – a single robot body capable of performing all core warehouse tasks (storage, retrieval, picking, packing, sorting, kitting, folding, bagging, boxing, etc) with superhuman performance replacing dozens of complex systems, enabling warehouses that operate at a fraction of the cost and complexity of an Amazon warehouse.

Prior to Nimble I was a PhD student at Stanford's Vision and Learning Lab (SVL) advised by "god-mother of AI", Dr. Fei-Fei Li, where my research focused on deep imitation learning for manipulation using data from teleoperation and wearable data collection exoskeletons. I received a M.S. in Robotics from the Robotics Institute at Carnegie Mellon and a B.S. from The Ohio State University. At CMU I developed the first low-cost quasi-direct-drive (QDD) actuators that catalyzed a new generation of low-cost dynamic legged robots like MIT mini cheetah and Unitree humanoids.

Nimble

Nimble builds autonomous industries powered by industry-generalist superhumanoids.

We're developing:

  • Robot AGI (General Robot Brain): robotic super intelligence that enables automation of dexterous loco-manipulation tasks across industries;
  • Industry-Generalist Superhumanoids: robot bodies designed and optimized to be general-purpose and superhuman in performing all or most tasks within a specific industry.

Today, Nimble builds fully autonomous warehouses powered by our intelligent generalist warehouse superhumanoid – the first robot capable of performing all core warehouse tasks including storage, retrieval, picking, packing, and sorting. Our proprietary superhumanoid replaces dozens of complex systems, enabling warehouses that operate at a fraction of the cost and complexity of today's leading systems from Symbotic and Amazon.

Nimble's Board of Directors includes AI Robotics pioneers Fei-Fei Li, Sebastian Thrun, and Marc Raibert.

FedEx and Nimble entered a strategic alliance in 2024 to build a large network of autonomous fulfillment centers with the potential to generate several billions in annual revenues within the next few years.
